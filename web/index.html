How Offer Guard works

An add-on to the cart recovery agent that decides, for every shopper, whether a discount will create a sale that would not happen otherwise.

About the model

What it predicts
For each abandoned cart, the chance the shopper buys with no offer, a 5% offer and a 10% offer. The difference is the effect an offer is expected to have.
Approach
A two-part uplift model. Boosted trees estimate whether the shopper would buy after a plain reminder; a regularised logistic model estimates how much a 5% or 10% offer adds. It is trained only on carts where the offer was assigned at random, pooled from comparable brands, so it learns what the offer caused rather than who happened to get one. A bigger offer can never lower the predicted chance of buying.
Signals it uses
Cart value and margin; why the shopper left (from the recovery agent's conversation, when it heard one); orders with the brand in the last 6 months; 30-day history of abandonments, offers received and full-price purchases; history from confirmed linked accounts.
Signals it never uses
Any shopper's identity or history from another merchant (other brands contribute only anonymous, randomised cart outcomes to training), the raw risk score, uncertain account links, or any shopper type label.
