How Offer Guard works

An add-on to the cart recovery agent that decides, for every shopper, whether a discount will create a sale that would not happen otherwise.

1

A shopper abandons a cart

The cart recovery agent is about to contact them by message or call.

2

An AI model estimates the effect of a discount

It predicts the chance of buying with and without an offer, and recommends one only if the extra sale is worth more than the discount.

3

The agent acts, and results are measured

A small random holdout gets no discount, measuring what discounts really add, so the model keeps learning.

About the model

What it predicts
For each abandoned cart, the chance the shopper buys with no offer, a 5% offer and a 10% offer. The difference is the effect an offer is expected to have.
Approach
An uplift model: it estimates how much an offer changes each shopper's behaviour, not just whether they will buy. Trained on outcomes, with the holdout providing the "no offer" comparison.
Signals it uses
Cart value and margin; why the shopper left (from the recovery agent's conversation); 30-day history of abandonments, offers received and full-price purchases; confirmed account links and the risk signal.
Signals it never uses
Data from other merchants, uncertain account links, or any shopper type label.
